About the art

The soul of Kalamkari

Kalamkari is a centuries-old Indian art of hand-painting - from kalam (pen) and kari (craftsmanship). Outlines drawn freehand. Colour layered with patience. Stories from temple, myth, and earth.

At The Painted Heritage we work from the sacred corridor of Tirupati - Sri Kalahasti, bringing that living tradition to canvas art for modern homes.

Kalam The pen that draws the line
Kari The craft of patient colour
